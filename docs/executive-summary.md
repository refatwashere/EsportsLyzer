# Executive Summary

## Product overview
EsportsLyzer is a live sports and esports intelligence platform designed to transform raw match data into readable, actionable match insights. The platform currently focuses on CS2 and is being structured so it can expand into football and tennis through a shared analytics foundation.

## Why it matters
The product addresses a common problem in live sports data: fans and analysts often have access to raw numbers, but not the context or narrative behind them. EsportsLyzer helps users understand what is happening in a match by combining live scores, momentum indicators, probability trends, and performance summaries in a single dashboard.

## Current product position
- CSAPI match results, player stats, and ranking-based estimates are connected
- Dashboard components and 30-second refresh flows are present; the provider does not guarantee live-match event coverage
- Visualization and analysis layers are active in the MVP
- Multi-sport support and personalization remain the next major expansion steps

## Business value
- Makes live match data easier to understand and follow
- Improves user engagement through visual storytelling
- Creates a reusable product foundation for multiple sports
- Keeps the architecture lightweight and deployable on free-tier infrastructure

## Roadmap at a glance
### Phase 1: Core Dashboard Foundation
Launch the MVP dashboard with live match data, charts, and key analytics views.

### Phase 2: Data Quality and Prediction Refinement
Improve reliability, model clarity, and narrative quality across the live experience.

### Phase 3: Multi-Sport Expansion
Support football and tennis through flexible data adapters and sport-aware views.

### Phase 4: Personalization and Engagement
Add auth, favorites, saved layouts, and user-specific customization.

### Phase 5: Advanced Analytics and Growth
Expand into deeper insights, exports, and platform-scale enhancements.

## Strategic recommendation
The best path is to continue refining the current CS2-first dashboard while keeping the architecture modular enough to support broader sports coverage. This balances short-term delivery with long-term product scalability.
