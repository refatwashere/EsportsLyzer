# Phase 5: Advanced Analytics and Growth

## Objective
Position EsportsLyzer as a deeper analytics platform with richer storytelling, stronger insights, and a clearer path toward scale and monetization.

## Outcomes
- Advanced insight generation for users
- More detailed analyst-grade visuals
- Stronger retention through storytelling and summaries
- Better platform extensibility for future growth

## Scope
### Advanced Analytics
- Player impact metrics and leaderboard tracking
- Team performance benchmarking
- Higher-quality match narrative summaries
- Match export, sharing, and recap tools

### Product Growth
- Add filters, presets, and saved analytical views
- Expand storytelling features for sports analysis
- Improve dashboard customization for power users

### Platform Maturity
- Evaluate deeper event analysis and AI-assisted commentary
- Refine deployment, monitoring, and production readiness
- Prepare the app for broader audience and data expansion

## Key Deliverables
- MVP and team impact tracking
- Match summary export or share features
- Deeper analytical overlays and dashboards
- Growth-ready product architecture

## Suggested Tasks
1. Build advanced impact and performance metrics
2. Add richer recap and export capabilities
3. Evaluate AI-assisted match commentary features
4. Improve analytics quality and presentation
5. Review deployment and support readiness

## Acceptance Criteria
- The app supports richer analyses beyond the live dashboard basics
- Match summaries are polished and actionable
- Users can interpret performance trends more effectively
- The product is ready for broader usage and expansion

## Risks
- Feature bloat without clear product value
- Advanced metrics becoming difficult to explain to users
- Growth features outpacing reliability and maintenance capacity

## Exit Criteria
Phase 5 is complete when the platform has evolved from a live dashboard into a compelling analytics and storytelling product with a strong foundation for long-term growth.
